# Northwind Fleet Billing and Refund Policy

A fictional billing policy used as part of the RAGProbe sample corpus.

## Free Trial

New customers may start a 14 day free trial of the Growth tier. The trial does not
require a credit card. At the end of the trial the account reverts to a read-only
state unless a paid subscription is started. Trial accounts are limited to 5
robots and do not include the DataVault integration.

## Cancellation

Subscriptions may be cancelled at any time from the billing settings page.
Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and the
account remains fully functional until that date. There is no cancellation fee on
any tier.

## Refund Eligibility

Monthly subscriptions are not refundable for partial months. Annual prepaid
subscriptions may be refunded on a pro-rata basis within the first 60 days of the
contract term. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 14
business days of approval.

## Billing Disputes

Invoice disputes must be raised within 30 days of the invoice date. The billing
team responds to disputes within 5 business days. Disputed amounts are not
considered overdue while the dispute is open, and no late fees accrue on the
disputed portion.
