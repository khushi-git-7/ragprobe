# Northwind Fleet Support Service Level Agreement

A fictional support SLA used as part of the RAGProbe sample corpus.

## Support Tiers

Standard support is included with the Starter and Growth tiers and operates during
business hours, 09:00 to 17:00 local time, Monday to Friday. Premium support is
included with Enterprise and operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Premium
customers are also assigned a named technical account manager.

## Response Times

Severity 1 incidents receive a first response within 1 hour on Premium support and
within 4 business hours on Standard support. Severity 2 receives a response within
4 hours on Premium and 1 business day on Standard. Severity 3 and 4 receive a
response within 2 business days on both tiers.

## Escalation Path

If a Severity 1 incident is not resolved within 4 hours, it is automatically
escalated to the engineering on-call lead. After 8 hours it is escalated to the
director of engineering. Customers may request a manual escalation at any time by
replying to the ticket with the word ESCALATE.

## Uptime Commitment

Northwind Fleet commits to 99.9 percent monthly uptime for the Growth tier and 99.95 percent
for the Enterprise tier. Uptime is measured against the control plane API.
Scheduled maintenance windows are excluded from the calculation and are announced
at least 7 days in advance.

## Service Credits

If monthly uptime falls below the committed level, affected customers receive a
service credit. A shortfall below 99.9 percent but above 99.0 percent earns a 10
percent credit. A shortfall below 99.0 percent earns a 25 percent credit. Service
credits must be claimed within 30 days of the end of the affected month.
