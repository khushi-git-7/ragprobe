Notes: known_failure. Retrieval is correct - the right chunk comes back at rank 1 - but that chunk lists Starter, Growth and Enterprise pricing in adjacent sentences, and the generator emits the two highest-scoring sentences. This case is the reason retrieval metrics alone are not enough: precision@k, recall@k, hit rate and MRR are all perfect here while the answer is still misleading.
